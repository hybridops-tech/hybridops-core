# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in HybridOps a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or level of
experience.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to
**[security@hybridops.tech](mailto:security@hybridops.tech)**. Reports are
handled confidentially and acknowledged within five business days.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may
face consequences determined through the project governance process.
